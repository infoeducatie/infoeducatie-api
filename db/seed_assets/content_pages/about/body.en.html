Founded in 1994, the competition is dedicated to high-school students interested in creating multidisciplinary software projects. It has four stages: school, local, county, and national. The national stage takes place in Focșani, Vrancea County.

Taking part in the competition

At every stage, students present projects they created regardless of their year of study (grades 9–12). Rankings from each stage decide who advances to the next one.
The final stage also includes a 24-hour open team challenge. Teams build a project around a given theme, combining product design, programming, and visual design.
Software engineering talks and learning sessions take place throughout the camp.

Participation requirements

Each county may participate with up to five students and one accompanying teacher, under the following conditions:
  • at least three projects
  • reasonable hardware and software requirements
